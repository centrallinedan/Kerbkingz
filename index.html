Kerb Kings

Throw the ball at the far kerb so it bounces back. You get 8 seconds per throw. Each level is a new street and tougher.

Move your finger to line up. Hold to charge, release in the green band.

Rebound passes the middle: 1 point. Catch it in the air: 2 points and you step to the middle.

Miss from the middle and you must run back before they hit you. If they miss, tap where they will be. Cars block the ball and flatten runners. Wind pushes the ball, so watch the arrows.

How to play

Slide to line up. Hold to charge, then let go in the middle of the green band.

Hit the far kerb. A rebound past the middle is 1 point. Catch it in the air on the green target for 2 and step to the middle.

Miss from the middle and you must run back or dodge. Cars, dogs and wind get in the way.

You get 8 seconds per throw. Three catches in a row earns a shield. Fill your put-off meter, then swipe up while the CPU lines up to put them off. The CPU can do the same to you.
